About

Everything JNU students need to know, in one place.

JNU Student Daily is an independent, student-run information platform. This is a demo build — the content is sample data for design preview, not real news.

The goal is simple: a fast, mobile-first daily that pulls together campus news, official notices, deadlines, events, opportunities and student issues — so you don't have to check ten noticeboards and five WhatsApp groups.

How we label what you read

Every item carries one of four honest labels, colour-coded across the site:

Official Notice — reproduced from an authority.
Reported News — checked by our desk.
Student Submission — sent by a reader, unverified.
Opinion — a personal viewpoint, not a report.

We never present an unverified student submission as confirmed news. When in doubt, check the original notice — every article links its source.
